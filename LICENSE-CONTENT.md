## What this licence covers

The explanatory, teaching material of Model Atlas, in English and in Chinese:

- **lesson text**: the titles, step texts and narration of the guided lessons, and their Chinese versions;
- **articles**: the longer explanations on model and training-concept pages, and the glossary definitions;
- **narration**: every sentence the lessons and views say about what the learner sees (including the templates
  the numbers are filled into);
- **figures and diagrams**: the drawings, charts, schematics and illustrations made for this project, whether they are
  image files or drawn by code from data written for the lesson.

This material lives both on the website (https://robintianqili.github.io/model-atlas/) and in this repository, often
inside source files (for example `site/src/lib/explainers/<slug>/lesson.ts` or the article components under
`site/src/lib/pages/`). In such files the text and figures are under CC BY 4.0 and the code around them is under the
MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

## What it does not cover

- **Code** (everything that runs, builds or tests the site and the models): MIT licence, see `LICENSE`.
- **Third-party material** (datasets, tokenizer vocabularies, model files derived from them, fonts, libraries, quoted
  facts from papers): each keeps its own licence. See `LICENSING.md` and `site/static/licenses.json`.
